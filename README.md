## 🎮 Helpful Tips for Best Results

Here are some tips to make your experience even smoother:

### 📌 Understanding Download Speeds
- **Faster internet = faster downloads** - the app can only go as fast as your connection allows
- **Large files take longer** - a 10-minute 4K video is bigger than a 3-minute 1080p one
- **Peak hours can be slower** - evenings and weekends might have more traffic

### 💾 Where Files Get Saved
By default, everything goes to your **C:\Users\[YourName]\Downloads** folder. You can change this in the settings (gear icon in the top-right corner).

### 🔄 Updating the App
fastmedia-downloader updates automatically. You'll see a small notification when a new version is available - just click "Update Now" and it handles the rest.

### 🆘 If Something Goes Wrong
- **"Error: Unsupported URL"** - This means the website isn't supported yet. Try a different site.
- **"Download failed"** - Check your internet connection and try again.
- **"File not found"** - The video was removed from the original site. Try another video.
